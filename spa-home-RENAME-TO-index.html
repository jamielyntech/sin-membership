Our Philosophy

Wellness isn't a single appointment. It's a relationship.

For approximately 37 years, Dr. Ron has helped people care for their health through a deeply personal combination of holistic wisdom, therapeutic touch, detoxification, advanced wellness technologies, and home-care support.

Some people come to preserve their wellness. Some come to restore it. Everyone deserves a thoughtful path toward greater wholeness.

Higher Purpose wasn't created simply to provide occasional treatments. It was created to help people build a more intentional relationship with their bodies, one appointment, one habit, one season at a time.
